Two impact models run in parallel. Bottom-up (Pathway 4) counts only the ~59 partners with named-client mappings — direct, named-client exposure. Top-down (Pathway 2) is a capacity proxy across the full active pipeline. Both are shown in the Client Impact sheet with a reconciliation note; they will differ by design and neither is scaled to match the other.

Editable lever: Change a partner's Phase in the Register sheet, re-run python3 build_data.py, and all bottom-up impact numbers recompute.
