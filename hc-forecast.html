HOT Capability Redesign
Draft · June 2026

A guide to reshaping the team over 18 months — and why the time to act is now

HOT Capability Redesign · Jun 2026 → Jun 2027
Of the 74 people in the team today, we believe there is a natural shift of around 40 — through partners leaving, natural attrition, and finding the right homes for team members. We propose replacing 30–40 of these, as long as costs remain low. Alongside this, we are building core partner relationships who can deliver services to clients at cost, in combination with clients themselves.
Context

Why we need to change

We are yet to fully embed the Human, Org and Tech operating model. This is primarily driven by the following challenges:

1
Partner cost imbalance — Our partner strategy for 2026 kicked off at the beginning of the year, taking ~25% of our workforce capacity but ~52% of our total spend.
2
Team profile vs future need — The team members we have internally are greatly experienced energy specialists, team leaders and ops managers who have inspired and delivered operations transformation for many years. Yet the capability required to deliver enduring transformational change for utilities is evolving, and the current team profile doesn't fully match it.
3
The age of AI — We are on the cusp of monumental AI transformation for clients — the continued need to create aligned and enabled utilities that can operate like modern tech and product companies.
4
End-to-end energy complexity — The integration of VPP, solar, heat pumps, C&I, water, distribution and beyond — and the innovation required to deliver them — demands a different kind of expertise than we have today.
The problem

With all of these challenges, we find ourselves misshapen

We have too many partners, creating a significant cost impact.
We don't have the required capabilities across each practice, and it's too challenging to get there without focus and effort.
Our business is moving to a regional focus enabled by global best practice — we haven't got our matrix working to make this effective.
The response
The time to think is over. We now need to act — strategically.
Reduce partner headcount and Kraken spend
Increase internal talent density
Reduce energy specialist and team leader focused roles as OTO continues to mature
Balance capability across practices
Get sharp on how each practice effectively adds value at a regional level

The Octopus Learning Model

What replaces 9 internal Learning staff

5 Octopus Energy L&D practitioners, deployed on a draw-down model — 2 × 3-month draws per 18-month migration.

Why
Replaces 9 internal Learning staff (£393,565/yr) at £184,167/yr — a saving of ~£209,000/yr. Octopus practitioners have first-hand Kraken platform experience. They know the system.
When
Activates Jan 2027. S Europe exits Jan 27 (aligned with Total France steady-state). N Europe exits Apr 27.
Cost model
£55k/yr per practitioner (midpoint £50–60k), engaged for 3 months only. Up to 6 practitioners live at any one time.
Per engagement: 3/12 × £55k = £13,750  ·  6 simultaneous: £82,500/draw  ·  Two draws per migration: ~£13,750/mo
Saving vs 9 internal L&D staff: ~£228,000/yr
Risk
S Europe Learning exits Jan 27 but Total France launches Sep 26 — a 4-month gap. Either pre-activate Octopus or delay S Europe exits to Apr 27.
